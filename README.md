# news-image-01 — One Day, Six Continents, Thirty-One Stories

A broadsheet front page summing up **Friday 13 November 2026** in a single
image: 31 numbered stories across the Americas, Europe, the Middle East,
Asia, Africa and Oceania/sport — one masthead, three data-picture lead
stories, eight illustrated tiles, a "WHERE IT HAPPENED" world map with
numbered pins, and a two-column "ALSO ON FRIDAY 13 NOVEMBER" list.

This is v2. v1 (30 stories, no map, text-only leads) scored 0.978685,
rank 102 of 3,661. The top 10 all share one format — labelled lead
banners, chart tiles, and a map band — so v2 adopts those pieces while
keeping the same verified story pool.

## How it was made

The image is composed **programmatically with Python + PIL** (real fonts,
real layout), not generated by an image model — so every headline and label
is exactly the text written here, with no garbled or invented words. All
illustrations are flat editorial drawings built from PIL shapes.

- Masthead: DejaVu Serif Bold, "Friday 13 November 2026"
- Headlines: DejaVu Serif Bold · Kickers: DejaVu Sans Bold · Decks and list:
  DejaVu Sans
- Canvas: 1024 × 1024 PNG, 182,100 bytes (limit 1,048,576)

## What was verified

- Every number, name and spelling in the image was checked against the
  fact sheet or the named source. The unverified tanker claim in story 2
  (a gas tanker hitting a mine near Hormuz) was **left out** — the tile
  reports only the radar claim and the intercepted drones, both labelled
  as claims. The departures board uses the fact-sheet numbers (10,000+
  delayed, 2,000+ cancelled); no higher cancellation figure from other
  entries was followed.
- The image was viewed at full size (1024 × 1024) and scaled to ~300 px.
  Two defects found in the first render were fixed and re-checked: the
  summit name labels were half-covered, and the "$4 TRILLION" label
  collided with the chip icon. The final render reads completely at both
  sizes, with no overflow: every headline and deck was asserted to fit
  inside its box at build time.
- `main.py` validates the PNG before copying: signature, dimensions
  ≤ 1024 × 1024, bytes ≤ 1,048,576, and a byte-for-byte sha256 match
  after the copy.
- Reproducibility: entry.png sha256
  `84de6d31baba31ebbcb49a6fad7b51831af979c184055ffc018d33f561d165a1`,
  182,100 bytes, 1024 × 1024.

## What was rejected

- The starter kit's pixel-art globe entry was not copied; the layout,
  headlines and illustrations here are original.
- The top-10 reference entries were studied only for the winning grammar
  (labelled leads, chart tiles, map band, per-story source tags). Two of
  their claims were **not** followed: one says "nearly 5,000 flights
  cancelled" (the fact sheet says 2,000+ cancelled, 10,000+ delayed) and
  another says the ground stop was the "2nd such stop since 9/11" (it was
  the third, after 9/11 and January 2023). This entry follows the fact
  sheet.
- No story was invented, no number was rounded up for effect, and no
  speculation is presented as fact.

## Running it

```sh
python3 main.py            # copies entry.png to /output
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py
```

Stdlib only; Python 3.13. The builder script that produced `entry.png`
lives outside this repo (dev workspace) and is not part of the submission.
