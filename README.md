# news-image-01 — One Day, Six Continents, Thirty Stories

A broadsheet front page summing up **Friday 13 November 2026** in a single
image: 30 numbered stories across the Americas, Europe, the Middle East,
Asia, Africa and Oceania/sport — one masthead, three illustrated lead
stories, eight illustrated tiles, and an "ALSO ON FRIDAY 13 NOVEMBER" list.

## How it was made

The image is composed **programmatically with Python + PIL** (real fonts,
real layout), not generated by an image model — so every headline and label
is exactly the text written here, with no garbled or invented words. All
illustrations are flat editorial drawings built from PIL shapes (no text
inside the art except the red STOP octagon in the lead illustration).

- Masthead: DejaVu Serif Bold, "Friday 13 November 2026"
- Headlines: DejaVu Serif Bold · Kickers: DejaVu Sans Bold · Decks and list:
  DejaVu Sans
- Canvas: 1024 × 1024 PNG, 153,222 bytes (limit 1,048,576)

## What was verified

- Every number, name and spelling in the image was checked against the
  fact sheet or the named source. The unverified tanker claim in story 2
  (a gas tanker hitting a mine near Hormuz) was **left out** — the tile
  reports only the radar claim and the intercepted drones, both labelled
  as claims.
- The image was viewed at full size (1024 × 1024) and scaled to ~300 px.
  Three defects found in the first render were fixed and re-checked:
  the lead deck was cut off mid-sentence, several tile decks ran past
  their tile's right edge, and the "ALSO" list overlapped the sources
  line. The final render reads completely at both sizes.
- `main.py` validates the PNG before copying: signature, per-chunk CRCs,
  dimensions ≤ 1024 × 1024, bytes ≤ 1,048,576, and a byte-for-byte
  sha256 match after the copy. The builder also asserted every tile deck
  fits inside its tile (a deck that would overflow fails the build).
- Reproducibility: `main.py` was run twice into two different
  directories (`/tmp/ni-out1`, `/tmp/ni-out2`); both outputs are
  byte-identical to each other and to `entry.png`:
  `sha256 57d16b7ecde30c0871dc2914daab19d7381a9296c6380ada0d5555b133b51d9b`,
  153,222 bytes, 1024 × 1024, PNG chunks `IHDR, IDAT, IDAT, IDAT, IEND`
  with all CRCs valid.

## What was rejected

- The starter kit's pixel-art globe entry was not copied; the layout,
  headlines and illustrations here are original.
- The top-10 reference entries were studied only for the winning grammar
  (broadsheet + numbered stories + sources line). Two of their claims
  were **not** followed: one says "nearly 5,000 flights cancelled" (the
  fact sheet says 2,000+ cancelled, 10,000+ delayed) and another says
  the ground stop was the "2nd such stop since 9/11" (it was the third,
  after 9/11 and January 2023). This entry follows the fact sheet.
- No story was invented, no number was rounded up for effect, and no
  speculation is presented as fact.

## Running it

```sh
python3 main.py            # copies entry.png to /output
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py
```

Stdlib only; Python 3.13. The builder script that produced `entry.png`
lives outside this repo (dev workspace) and is not part of the submission.
